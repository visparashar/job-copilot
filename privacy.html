Job Copilot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Summary

Job Copilot does not collect, transmit, sell or share your personal data. Everything you enter stays in your own browser.

What the extension stores

  • Your profile details that you type or import from your resume (for example name, email, phone, address, links, work and education details, and answers to common application questions).
  • A copy of your resume file, if you import one, so it can be attached to applications.
  • Answer snippets you create, your settings, and a simple count of how many times you used autofill.

This data is saved with the browser's built-in extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your device. It is not synced to any server operated by the developer, and the developer has no access to it.

How the data is used

Your data is used only to fill job application forms when you click Autofill (or use the keyboard shortcut). The extension reads the labels of form fields on the page to decide which of your details belongs in each field. When it fills a form, the information goes only to that form on the website you are applying to — exactly as if you had typed it yourself. The extension never submits a form for you.

Resume files are read on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere by the extension, except into an application form's upload field when you choose to autofill.

What the extension does not do

  • No accounts, analytics, tracking, advertising or remote code.
  • No sale or transfer of data to third parties.
  • No use of your data for anything other than filling your job applications.

Donations

The “Buy me a coffee” button opens the developer's donation page in a new tab. Any payment happens on that third-party site under its own privacy policy; the extension does not send it any of your data.

Deleting your data

Open the extension, go to Settings → Delete all data, or simply uninstall the extension. Both remove everything it stored.
