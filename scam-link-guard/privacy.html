Scam Link Guard — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Summary

Scam Link Guard checks links on your own computer. It does not collect, send, sell or share what you read, the links you see, or anything about you.

What the extension reads

On the supported chat, email and social-media websites, the extension reads the web addresses of links on the page and a short piece of the message around each link, so it can decide whether the link looks like a scam. This happens entirely inside your browser. The text is used only for that check and is not stored or sent anywhere.

What it stores on your computer

  • Your settings (on/off and language) and the sites you chose to trust.
  • Simple counters (links checked, warnings shown, clicks stopped) and a list of the last 25 warned web addresses, shown in the extension's popup.
  • A public list of known phishing domains.

All of this is kept in the browser's extension storage on your device. The developer has no access to it.

Network use

Once a day the extension downloads the public, open-source Phishing.Database list of known phishing domains from GitHub. This is a plain download of the whole list: no information about you, your browsing or the links you see is included in the request. Links are never sent to any server to be checked.

What the extension does not do

  • No accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or remote code.
  • No sale or transfer of data to anyone.
  • No use of data for anything other than warning you about scam links.

Donations

The “Buy me a coffee” button opens the developer's donation page in a new tab. Any payment happens on that third-party site under its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the extension removes everything it stored.
